Glass Lantern Studios

Glass Lantern Studios is run by co-founders Dwayne Alexander (he/him) and Alex Gamboa (she/her), equal partners in everything we make.

We don't hand you the horizon. We hand you a lantern.

Who I am

Musician, storyteller and studio producer, with over four decades in roots rock, acoustic blues and Celtic folk. I started at nine, playing guitar beside my father's fiddle, and we played together for twenty-six years across Canada.

I’m not a therapist. I’m someone who’s lived it, and music is how I’ve made it through. Everything I make comes from real life, and Glass Lantern Studios is built on that lived experience, so nobody has to face the hard stuff alone.

The studio

Glass Lantern Studios is a music, spoken word and storytelling house, and a harbour. Concept albums and songs that give honest shape to the heavy, unspoken things, and company for the ordinary hours.

  • The Glass Lantern — the band, and the music catalogue so far
  • Tales from the Sound Hole — true stories, told in my own voice
  • The Calavera Hour — short spoken word over fingerstyle blues, about the people we miss
  • The Raven's Wire — spoken word turned into music across many sounds
  • Obsidian Lantern — our record label, turning real people's stories into songs. Tell us your story

Healthy Peruvian Kitchen with Chef Mamá Rockera

Comfort, tradition and resilience

Meet Betty, Alex’s mom, known in our kitchen as Chef Mamá Rockera. For Betty, cooking has never been only about food. Growing up in Lima, Peru, she learned early that a kitchen can be a place of warmth and connection. She has come through hard seasons with her family, and she knows how a shared table and a home-cooked meal can help hold people together.

Now she brings that heart to Glass Lantern Studios: authentic, healthy Peruvian cooking made with soul, every step explained so you can make it at home. The best recipes, she says, are seasoned with a lifetime of stories.

Every recipe comes three ways:

  • A cooking video to follow along with
  • The written recipe, step by step
  • The why behind every ingredient and process

Coming soon: the Chef Mamá Rockera cookbook. Her recipes in one book, each paired with its video.

Cooked with love, the Peruvian way.
