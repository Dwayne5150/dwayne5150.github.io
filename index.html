Who I am

Musician, storyteller and studio producer, with over four decades in roots rock, acoustic blues and Celtic folk. I started at nine, playing guitar beside my father's fiddle, and we played together for twenty-six years across Canada.

I have a background in music therapy, and everything I make comes from real life. Glass Lantern is built on lived experience, so nobody has to face the hard stuff alone.

The studio

Glass Lantern Studios is a music, spoken word and storytelling house, and a harbour. Concept albums and songs that give honest shape to the heavy, unspoken things, and company for the ordinary hours.

  • The Glass Lantern — the band, and the music catalogue so far
  • Tales from the Sound Hole — true stories, told in my own voice
  • The Calavera Hour — short spoken word over fingerstyle blues, about the people we miss
  • The Raven's Wire — spoken word turned into music across many sounds
  • Obsidian Lantern — my record label, turning real people's stories into songs. Tell me your story
