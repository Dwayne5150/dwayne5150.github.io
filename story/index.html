Tell us the story. We'll make it into something real.

You don't need to be a musician or an artist. If you've lived it and it marked you, it's worth telling. Dark or light, big or small, as long as it's true.

Nothing you type here is sent anywhere. When you're ready, email it to Dwayne and Alex from the bottom of the page. Your draft stays saved on this device so you can come back to it; press Clear the form at the bottom when you're done, especially on a shared computer.

A sample

A story we made into a song

“My dad played fiddle his whole life, and I spent 26 years beside him on guitar. The night I remember most was an East Coast kitchen party, with the house so full you couldn’t move. Someone wanted to step dance, so the man of the house took his front door off its hinges and laid it on the floor. Dad and I tore through jigs and reels while dress shoes hammered that door. He’s gone now, but I can still hear it.”
— Dwayne

It became “One Tune More.”

Take the door down off the hinges, Lay it on the kitchen floor, Let the dress shoes do the talking, Dad, play me one tune more.
