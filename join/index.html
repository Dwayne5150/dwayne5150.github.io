Before you join

Welcome to the Lighthouse Sessions. Take two minutes to read this, then come on in.

What this is

A free hour of music, stories and company to start the day, hosted by Dwayne and Alex. It’s peer support from people who’ve lived it. It isn’t therapy. It’s company, so nobody does the hard trip alone.

Room guidelines

  • Cameras are optional. Come as you are.
  • No pressure to talk. Listening is joining.
  • Be kind. Everyone here knows the rough water.
  • What’s shared here stays here. No recording, no screenshots.

If you’re in danger right now, please reach out to a crisis line. They’re free and open 24 hours a day.

Canada and the US: call or text 988. Peru: call 113, option 5. Anywhere else: findahelpline.com.

Glass Lantern is peer support, not emergency care.
